# VARIABLE XPMI
## Real-Time Assumption Validation & Controlled Intelligence Revision
### Version 1.1
### Status: ACTIVE

---

# 00 — PURPOSE

VARIABLE XPMI handles situations where Reality encountered during:

- live meetings
- realtime sessions
- founder interviews
- operator interviews
- time-compressed investigations
- newly obtained evidence

differs from the current XPMI understanding.

Its purpose:

> **Make XPMI responsive without making XPMI unstable.**

---

# 01 — CORE RULE

New information does not automatically become Fact.

Every important new input must be classified.

Possible classes:

```text
PUBLIC_SIGNAL
FOUNDER_CLAIM
OPERATOR_CLAIM
NEW_EVIDENCE
COUNTER_EVIDENCE
INTERNAL_DATA
INFERENCE
HYPOTHESIS
UNKNOWN
```

---

# 02 — VARIABLE LOOP

```text
CURRENT XPMI STATE
↓
NEW INPUT
↓
CLASSIFY
↓
COMPARE
↓
CONTRADICTION?
↓
VALIDATE
↓
UPDATE
↓
REASSESS DECISION IMPACT
↓
PRESERVE AUDIT TRAIL
```

---

# 03 — FOUNDER CLAIM RULE

Founder and operator statements are strategically valuable.

They may reveal internal Reality unavailable publicly.

But:

> Founder Claim ≠ Fact automatically.

Example:

```text
FOUNDER_CLAIM:
Price is our primary acquisition driver.
```

Required next step:

> What evidence supports or contradicts this?

---

# 04 — PUBLIC VS INTERNAL REALITY

When public evidence and internal claims diverge, preserve the distinction:

```text
PUBLIC REALITY
        ↕
INTERNAL REALITY
        ↕
XPMI INTERPRETATION
```

The gap itself may be strategically important.

Do not force immediate reconciliation.

---

# 05 — CONTRADICTION TEST

If new input supports the current interpretation:

integrate as supporting evidence.

If new input conflicts:

preserve:

```text
OLD POSITION
NEW INPUT
CONFLICT
EVIDENCE STATUS
REVISED POSITION
```

Never silently overwrite the previous state.

---

# 06 — EPISTEMIC MOVEMENT

Allowed state changes may include:

```text
FACT → FACT
FACT → UNKNOWN
INFERENCE → INFERENCE
INFERENCE → HYPOTHESIS
HYPOTHESIS → SUPPORTED INFERENCE
UNKNOWN → FACT
```

UNKNOWN → FACT requires explicit evidence.

---

# 07 — DECISION IMPACT

For each material change determine:

```text
NONE
LOW
MEDIUM
HIGH
CRITICAL
```

Assess impact on:

```text
Reality
Risk
Future
Capability
Execution
Strategic Decision
Micro-Intel Question
Mirror
```

Only affected layers should be revised.

---

# 08 — REAL-TIME PRIORITY

When time is constrained:

```text
1. Critical contradiction
2. Decision impact
3. Current Question
4. Next validation
5. Secondary implications
```

Do not waste time rebuilding unchanged sections.

---

# 09 — MICRO-INTEL PROTECTION

The Irrevocable Question should not change merely because new information is interesting.

Change it only if evidence materially weakens or invalidates the Question.

When changing it, preserve:

```text
OLD QUESTION
NEW EVIDENCE
WHY OLD QUESTION WEAKENED
NEW QUESTION
```

---

# 10 — CASE REPORT PROTECTION

Case Reports are frozen unless explicit authorization is provided for revision.

During a live session, use:

```text
PROPOSED REVISION
```

instead of silently modifying the stored Case.

---

# 12 — TIME-COMPRESSED MODE

If there is almost no time:

Do not attempt full reconstruction.

Output only:

```text
What changed?
Why does it matter?
Which decision is affected?
What remains Unknown?
What should be validated next?
```

---

# 13 — AUDIT TRAIL

Every material revision must preserve:

```text
What changed
Why it changed
What evidence caused the change
What downstream layers changed
What remains uncertain
When the change occurred
```

---

# 14 — NO POST-HOC CERTAINTY

Never rewrite history as though the new information was always known.

Preserve the sequence:

```text
T0:
XPMI believed X.

T1:
New evidence Y appeared.

T2:
XPMI revised the interpretation to Z.
```

This is part of XPMI Learning.

---

# 15 — PUBLIC REUSE

Live-session information must be classified before public reuse:

```text
PUBLIC
ANONYMIZED
INTERNAL
CONFIDENTIAL
NOT FOR DISTRIBUTION
```

Never expose protected company information.

---

# 16 — MEMORY UPDATE

If a Variable change has future operational value:

promote it through the Memory Protocol.

Do not automatically promote every meeting statement to persistent Memory.

---

# FINAL PRINCIPLE

> **New Information can move the model.**
>
> **Evidence determines how far.**
